The mandate

Deciding what an agent is allowed to do

I own the agent and tool standards that IG Labs and client delivery teams build against, and the promotion-gate policy that decides what reaches production. Behavioral reliability, runtime guardrails, adversarial evaluation, identity and access.

I ran our ISO/IEC 42001 gap assessment and mapped the control framework to the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and model risk management expectations. That last one matters more than it sounds: the April 2026 interagency model risk revision places agentic systems outside its formal scope, so banks now carry an obligation with no rulebook attached.

I also direct model, vendor, and build-versus-buy decisions across three AI platforms with $2.4M in governed annual spend, which has cut cost per task between 26% and 72% depending on the use case, without moving the quality and reliability gates.

Regulated finance, 2007 – 2024

Ten years shipping models where the examiner shows up

Most of this work is proprietary and there is no repository to point at, which is the honest situation for anyone who has built inside a bank.

As VP of Data and AI for Truist's wholesale banking division I led a 50-person engineering organization across software, ML, and data science, and founded Truist Foundry, the division's AI innovation lab. We built the derivatives compliance platform from zero across Dodd-Frank, Volcker, CFTC, SEC, and ISDA IM, and delivered the post-merger payments consolidation and SWIFT ISO 20022 migration, cross-border and multi-rail, with sanctions and AML screening through the middle of two banks becoming one.

The models mattered too. A multivariate Gaussian risk analytics dashboard became the CIB divisional standard. Counterparty risk and probability-of-default modeling on Moody's and Fitch data cut measured risk exposure by 40%. Client segmentation for derivatives traders lifted profitable trade rate 28%.

Before Truist I spent nine years embedded at SunTrust through Quinnox, leading a 30-person team on capital markets delivery, running the AI/ML innovation lab, and building the bank's first production ML systems: lead scoring in 2016, document summarization in 2017, a conversational agent in 2018. Earlier still, application development for Crédit Agricole out of Hong Kong and GIC out of Singapore.
